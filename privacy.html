Blocky Block Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

Blocky Block is a single-player puzzle game. The game itself has no accounts and keeps your game data on your device. To stay free, it shows ads provided by Google AdMob, and Google collects some data to show and measure those ads, as described below.

What the app stores

The app saves the following only on your device, so you can continue where you left off:

  • Your best score
  • Your current game in progress
  • How many of your recent games ended early in a row, so the next game can start a little easier
  • Your settings (for example, whether sound and vibration are on)

This data never leaves your device. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Ads

Blocky Block shows three kinds of ads:

  • an optional video ad you can choose to watch to continue a game once,
  • an occasional full-screen ad between games, and
  • sometimes a banner ad under the pause menu.

To report an ad that seems inappropriate, tap Report an ad in the pause menu or email the address below.

These ads are provided by Google AdMob. To deliver ads, limit how often you see them, measure their performance and prevent fraud, Google's ad software in the app may collect:

  • device identifiers, including Apple's advertising identifier (IDFA) only if you allow tracking when asked
  • your IP address and the approximate location derived from it
  • information about your device (model, operating system, language) and the app
  • how you interact with ads (for example, whether an ad was shown or tapped)
  • diagnostic information about ad performance and errors

Blocky Block itself does not receive or store this data. Google handles it under its own policies:

  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services
  • Google Privacy Policy

Your choices

  • Tracking: the app asks for permission before allowing tracking across other companies' apps and websites. If you decline, ads are still shown, but they aren't personalized using your advertising identifier. You can change this at any time in iPhone Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • European Economic Area, UK and Switzerland: you are asked for consent before ads are personalized, and you can change your choice at any time with Ad privacy choices in the pause menu.
  • Only ads rated suitable for all ages are requested.

What the app does not do

  • No accounts or sign-in
  • No analytics or crash-reporting services of its own
  • No selling of personal data
  • No access to your contacts, photos, location services, camera, or microphone

Children

Blocky Block is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If a future version changes how data is handled, this policy will be updated and the date above will change.
